Çocuklar. Oyun korku temaları içerir ve 13 yaş altındaki çocuklara yönelik değildir. Çocuklardan bilerek veri toplamayız.

Değişiklikler. Bu politika güncellenirse yeni sürüm bu sayfada yayımlanır.

English

03:17 — Mavicam Apartments (the "Game") is developed by Embe Game Studio.

Data we collect. The Game itself does not collect personal data. It does not ask for an account, sign-in, location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

Ads (mobile free version only). In the free version, the "Hint" and "Second Chance" features are unlocked with short rewarded videos that the player chooses to watch. The Game never shows ads on its own; there are no banners or interstitials. These videos are served by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and the ad networks it mediates. These services may process information such as the device advertising identifier (IDFA/GAID), IP address (approximate location), device model and operating system to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Before the first video you are asked whether ads may be personalised; you can change this at any time in Settings. On iOS, tracking permission (ATT) is only requested if you choose personalised ads. Players who buy the full version see no ads at all. Details: Unity Privacy Policy.

Data stored on your device. Game progress (current night, found documents, endings seen, night grades), settings, your ad preference and your full-game entitlement are stored only in a save file on your device. This file is never sent to us. Deleting the Game also deletes this data.

Purchases. The full-game purchase is made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Your payment details never reach us; the transaction is governed by the respective store's privacy policy. Unity IAP is used to communicate the purchase with the store.

Children. The Game contains horror themes and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes. If this policy is updated, the new version will be published on this page.

Contact. For questions: embegamestudio@gmail.com
